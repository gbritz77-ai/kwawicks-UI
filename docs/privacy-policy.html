Privacy Policy for KwaWicks

Last updated: 20 April 2026

KwaWicks ("we", "our", or "us") operates the KwaWicks mobile application (the "App"). This page informs you of our policies regarding the collection, use, and disclosure of personal data when you use our App.

1. Information We Collect

The App collects the following types of information to provide its services:

  • Account information: Name, email address, and role within your organisation, used for authentication via Amazon Cognito.
  • Business data: Delivery orders, invoices, client records, and inventory data entered by authorised users in the course of their work.
  • Device information: Device type and operating system version, collected automatically to ensure compatibility.
  • Camera: The App requests camera access to allow users to photograph and upload payment receipts and delivery documentation.

2. How We Use Your Information

We use the collected information solely to:

  • Authenticate and authorise users within the KwaWicks platform.
  • Enable the management of delivery orders, invoices, and inventory.
  • Generate and share business documents such as invoices and statements.
  • Store payment receipt images securely in Amazon S3.

3. Data Sharing

We do not sell, trade, or rent your personal information to third parties. Data is shared only with the following service providers who process it on our behalf:

  • Amazon Web Services (AWS): Cloud infrastructure, storage, and authentication.

4. Data Retention

Business records are retained for as long as required by applicable South African law and your organisation's operational needs. You may request deletion of your account data by contacting us.

5. Security

We implement industry-standard security measures including encrypted data transmission (HTTPS), JWT-based authentication, and role-based access control. No method of transmission over the internet is 100% secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security.

6. Children's Privacy

The App is intended for use by business professionals only and is not directed at children under the age of 18. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children.

7. Your Rights

You have the right to access, correct, or request deletion of your personal data. To exercise these rights, contact us at the address below.

8. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by updating the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
